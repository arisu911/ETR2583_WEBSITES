When students grind past midnight, brain fatigue triggers intense hunger spikes. However, campus infrastructure completely fails them.

10:00 PM LOCK

💸
+RM 6.00 – RM 8.00

Students are forced into three terrible compromises:
  • Soggy Local Snacks: Convenience shop crackers lose crunch rapidly in humidity, delivering zero dopamine satisfaction.
  • Delivery App Extortion: Paying RM15 total for a RM7 late-night order is economically unsustainable for students.

⚡ MARKET VOID: A high-margin, ultra-crispy snack delivered directly to dorm rooms without exorbitant delivery surcharges.
