// PROBLEM & MISSION

Solving the Midnight Campus Void.

The Friction

The 11:00 PM Cafe Shutter

The campus cafe shutters strictly at 11:00 PM, leaving midnight study pods stranded. Food delivery aggregators impose RM6–RM8 surge fees, while loose store snacks go soft and soggy in coastal hostel humidity. Late-night focus demands immediate, reliable sustenance without unreasonable delivery markups.

The Solution

The Sealed Standard

Breakout Bites eliminates kitchen prep chaos by sourcing high-demand confectionery in bulk, inspecting and portioning each batch under strict hygiene standards, and seal them into food-grade stand-up ziplock pouches so the crunch stays completely locked in. Maximum crunch preservation, zero aggregator markups, and rapid hostel handoffs.
