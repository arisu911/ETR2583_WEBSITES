I am currently studying for a Diploma in Accounting at Kolej Profesional MARA Bandar Penawar. While accounting is my formal academic field of study, my strongest personal interests center on financial markets, macroeconomics, portfolio management, quantitative finance, and investment analysis—actively studying global macroeconomics, capital market structures, liquidity cycles, and multi-asset behavior across international indices and commodity markets, with a clear focus toward investment management and applied economics as a long-term path.

Outside my coursework, I build data and finance applications. Working with Python, SQL, Excel, and Power BI alongside modern AI development tooling like Google AI Studio and Antigravity IDE on GitHub, I build projects exploring financial market behavior, multi-asset portfolios, and risk visualization.

Breakout Bites Studio was created as a practical, campus-oriented project—bringing together digital marketing, social commerce, and straightforward day-to-day operations to solve late-night hostel cravings. Beyond finance and technology, I enjoy anime, gaming, automotive engineering, and creative digital media. I like building things, experimenting with ideas, and connecting different disciplines together.

Accounting is my formal academic education, while finance, technology, and building projects are where my curiosity has expanded.
